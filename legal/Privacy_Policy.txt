Privacy Policy
Last Updated: March 24, 2026
PILR (“we,” “our,” or “us”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit or make a purchase from our Shopify-powered website (the “Site”).
1. Personal Information We Collect
When you visit the Site, we may automatically collect certain information about your device, including:
web browser;
IP address;
time zone;
cookies installed on your device.
Additionally, as you browse the Site, we may collect information about:
the pages or products you view;
the websites or search terms that referred you to the Site;
how you interact with the Site.
We refer to this information as Device Information.
When you make a purchase or attempt to make a purchase through the Site, we may collect certain information from you, including:
name;
billing address;
shipping address;
payment information, processed securely through Shopify and its payment providers;
email address;
phone number.
We refer to this information as Order Information.
When we talk about Personal Information in this Privacy Policy, we are referring to both Device Information and Order Information.
2. How We Use Your Personal Information
We use Order Information to:
process payments through Shopify;
fulfill orders and arrange shipping;
send order confirmations and updates;
communicate with you;
screen orders for potential risk or fraud.
We use Device Information to:
improve and optimize our Site;
analyze customer browsing behavior;
assess the success of marketing and advertising campaigns;
understand how users interact with the Site;
help detect fraud or security issues.
3. Email Marketing
If you opt in to receive emails, we may send you:
product updates;
promotions and special offers;
marketing communications.
You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the “unsubscribe” link in our emails or by contacting us at support@pilrsystems.com.
Our marketing emails are intended to comply with applicable U.S. law, including the CAN-SPAM Act. This means that our commercial emails are intended to include accurate sender information, non-misleading subject lines, a clear way to opt out of future marketing emails, and a valid physical postal address.
Even if you unsubscribe from marketing emails, we may still send you non-promotional communications related to your orders, purchases, shipping, returns, customer service requests, or important updates about our policies or services.
