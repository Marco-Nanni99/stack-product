Our Story

Built from a routine.
Designed for the real world.

Two friends, one shared problem — and a system built to solve it.

01 — The Starting Point

We lived the same way.

We met at UCLA and became close for one reason: we lived the same way. Same major, same schedule, same interests, same routine — to the gym, to class, to everything. Routine wasn't something we forced. It was just who we were.

Supplements became a serious part of that. Not because we were chasing extremes, but because we understood what consistency actually builds over time and the science fascinated us.

02 — The Break in Routine

Travel broke it every time.

Upon graduating and starting to work corporate jobs we both realized that travel broke it every time. Powders didn't fit in pill containers. Ziploc bags spilled. Cheap organizers cracked. We realized we had the same problem. Most of the time, we just gave up — and starting over is never as easy as it sounds.

Nothing existed for the way we actually lived. It was either ziplocs, a Monday-through-Sunday undersized pill case from the drugstore, carrying full-size tubs that took up half a bag, or just skipping it altogether. Everything was a workaround. Nothing was a system.

03 — Designing the System

So we built one.

Something that could handle pills and powders together. Something modular — because some people carry creatine and that's it, and some people carry ten things. Something that fits your routine, not a generic version of it.

Nine prototypes in, we stopped trying to improve what already existed and started designing around how people actually live. The result is three pods, each with a purpose — and a system that finally makes consistency portable.

We didn't cut corners on materials either. Tritan for the pods — BPA-free, food-safe, built to last. An aluminum shell that feels like it belongs on a bathroom counter or in a gym bag, not in a dollar bin. This isn't something you replace. It's something you keep.

04 — The Standard

Consistency is what drives results.

Everyone who takes their health seriously knows this. The problem was never the routine — it was that nothing was built to protect it.

Pilr is. No matter where you are.

— Andrea & Justin
